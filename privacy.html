Data collection

We use Letter (lettervisit.com) to count visits and run A/B tests. For visitors outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Letter may also store data in the browser and use a third-party service to identify visitors, unless the browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

When an automated agent reads this site, or a visitor arrives from an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, our server also sends Letter that request's IP address, browser headers (never cookies or login credentials) and page address. This applies in every country.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics on every page, and Vercel Web Analytics on our /vegas page, to measure visits and page performance.

Information submitted through our forms (name, email, company, role, store format, number of stores, country and any message) is used solely to respond to your inquiry. Submissions are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms.
